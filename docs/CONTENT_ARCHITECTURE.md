# Content Architecture

## Unit grouping requested by the learner

### Chapter 0.0 — Foundation refresher
Real-number system, signed arithmetic, fractions, algebra vocabulary, terms/factors, order of operations, exponent rules, equation language, and calculator entry.

### Chapters 1–2 — Algebra engine
- radicals and rational exponents;
- absolute value with even roots;
- GCF and factoring identities;
- rational expressions and cancellation of factors;
- rationalization and conjugates;
- equation classifications and domain restrictions;
- quadratic methods;
- complex numbers and powers of `i`;
- rational/radical/absolute-value equations;
- linear, compound, polynomial, and rational inequalities.

### Chapters 3–4 — Graph and function engine
- coordinate system and quadrants;
- distance and midpoint;
- slope, point-slope, slope-intercept, standard form;
- parallel/perpendicular lines and perpendicular bisectors;
- circles and completing the square;
- function notation, domain/range, composition, inverse;
- graph features, transformations, even/odd;
- polynomial and rational functions.

### Chapter 5 — Current priority
- composition and inverse review;
- exponential functions and graph behavior;
- discrete/continuous compound interest;
- logarithmic form and exponential form;
- log domain/range and one-to-one equations;
- properties of logarithms;
- exponential and logarithmic equations.

## Question-family anatomy

Every family Markdown file contains:

1. identity and source references;
2. professor surface construction;
3. underlying mathematical structure;
4. lecture-level form;
5. assessment-level form;
6. hidden prerequisite chain;
7. recognition clues;
8. first decision;
9. decision path;
10. restrictions/checks;
11. variation dimensions;
12. difficulty ladder;
13. common errors and targeted feedback;
14. expected answer presentation;
15. mastery standard.

## Progression layers

- L0 Foundation language
- L1 Direct concept
- L2 Guided procedure
- L3 Representation change
- L4 Mixed prerequisite
- L5 Professor style
- L6 Timed assessment
- L7 Cumulative/final transfer

Difficulty is not increased by random larger numbers. It increases by controlled changes:

- less explicit wording;
- more representations;
- more hidden prerequisites;
- more branches/solutions;
- domain or extraneous checks;
- multipart dependency;
- stricter answer form;
- reduced scaffolding;
- time pressure.

## Source traceability

A generated item must be able to answer:

- Which source question(s) established this surface pattern?
- Which lecture section established the core method?
- Which solution source established the expected answer form?
- What was varied, and why is the variation still faithful?
