# Supplemental Raw Sources

The 53 Markdown files under `sources/` are the canonical, usable Math 153 corpus.

Commit `d81cc96cdffe75f99a20c45ffb04d97d7cc72f6f` contains 99 screenshots under
`math153/`. They are **recoverable supplemental raw sources**. They are not merged into the
current branch and are not required by the Markdown ingestion pipeline. Recovery and visual
pairing are a separate operation; the Markdown files must not be rewritten during that work.

The filename-only PDF and screenshot lists are retained under
`data/legacy/raw-source_inventory/`. They document unavailable legacy binaries and are not the
canonical manifest.
