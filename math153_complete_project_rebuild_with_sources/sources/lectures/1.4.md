## Page 1 --- Fractional Expressions

A **fractional expression** is a fraction whose numerator and
denominator are algebraic expressions.

A special case is **rational expressions**, where the numerator and
denominator are polynomials.

### Fractional-expression example

\[ `\frac{12-y}{\sqrt[3]{x}-4}`{=tex} \]

### Rational-expression example

\[ `\frac{x^2+5x-3y}{y^3-x^2}`{=tex} \]

Problems involving rational expressions usually ask you to add,
subtract, multiply, or divide them and/or simplify by cancelling common
factors from the numerator and denominator.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Page 2 --- Simplifying and Combining Rational Expressions

### Simplify

\[ `\frac{12+r-r^2}{r^3+3r^2}`{=tex} \]

Factor the denominator:

\[ r^3+3r^2=r\^2(r+3) \]

Rewrite the numerator:

\[ 12+r-r^2=-r^2+r+12 \]

Factor:

\[ -r\^2+r+12=(-r-3)(r-4) \]

Therefore,

\[ `\frac{12+r-r^2}{r^3+3r^2}`{=tex} =
`\frac{(-r-3)(r-4)}{r^2(r+3)}`{=tex} \]

### Combine and simplify

\[
`\frac{6}{x(3x-2)}`{=tex}+`\frac{5}{3x-2}`{=tex}-`\frac{2}{x^2}`{=tex}
\]

The notes identify the least common denominator as

\[ `\boxed{\operatorname{LCD}=x^2(3x-2)}`{=tex} \]

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Page 3 --- Division of Rational Expressions

Combine and simplify:

\[ `\frac{a^3-8}{a^2-4}`{=tex}`\div`{=tex}`\frac{a}{a^3+8}`{=tex} \]

Change division to multiplication by the reciprocal:

\[ `\frac{a^3-8}{a^2-4}`{=tex}`\cdot`{=tex}`\frac{a^3+8}{a}`{=tex} \]

Useful factorizations:

\[ a^2-4=a^2-2\^2=(a-2)(a+2) \]

\[ a^3-8=a^3-2^3=(a-2)(a^2+2a+4) \]

\[ a^3+8=a^3+2^3=(a+2)(a^2-2a+4) \]

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Page 4 --- Factoring Before Simplifying

Combine and simplify:

\[ `\frac{x^2-4}{3x^2-5x+2}`{=tex} `\cdot`{=tex}
`\frac{9x^4-6x^3+4x^2}{27x^4+8x}`{=tex} \]

The notes factor the pieces:

\[ 9x^2-4=(3x)^2-2\^2=(3x-2)(3x+2) \]

\[ 9x^4-6x^3+4x^2=x^2(9x\^2-6x+4) \]

\[ 3x\^2-5x+2 = 3x\^2-3x-2x+2 \]

\[ =3x(x-1)-2(x-1) \]

\[ =(3x-2)(x-1) \]

\[ 27x^4+8x=x(27x^3+8) \]

and uses sum/difference-of-cubes and common-factor identities to
simplify. The boxed result in the notes is

\[ `\boxed{\frac{x}{x-1}}`{=tex} \]

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Page 5 --- Rationalizing a Numerator

For an expression of the form

\[ `\frac{\sqrt{x+h}-\sqrt{x}}{h}`{=tex}, \]

the notes say to **rationalize the numerator**.

Use the conjugate:

\[ `\sqrt{x+h}`{=tex}+`\sqrt{x}`{=tex} \]

Multiplying by the conjugate makes the numerator a difference of
squares:

\[
(`\sqrt{x+h}`{=tex}-`\sqrt{x}`{=tex})(`\sqrt{x+h}`{=tex}+`\sqrt{x}`{=tex})
=(x+h)-x=h \]

After cancellation:

\[ `\boxed{\frac{1}{\sqrt{x+h}+\sqrt{x}}}`{=tex} \]

## Page 6 --- Additional Rationalization Examples

The notes ask what happens if the numerator contains cube roots:

\[ `\frac{\sqrt[3]{x+h}-\sqrt[3]{x}}{h}`{=tex} \]

A second worked expression uses fractional exponents. The notes simplify

\[ `\frac{3x^2(2x+5)^{1/2}-x^3(2x+5)^{-1/2}}{2x+5}`{=tex} \]

by multiplying to obtain a common exponent in the numerator:

## Page 7 --- Final Factoring

Factor the numerator:

\[ 5x^3+15x^2=5x\^2(x+3) \]

Therefore,

\[ `\boxed{
\frac{5x^2(x+3)}{(2x+5)^{3/2}}
}`{=tex} \]
