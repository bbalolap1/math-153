## Q.1

\[ 3\|3x-1\|-2`\ge4`{=tex} \]

## Q.4

A point lies on the perpendicular bisector of a segment exactly when it
is equidistant from the segment's endpoints.

Distance formula: \[ d(P_1,P_2) =
`\sqrt{(x_1-x_2)^2+(y_1-y_2)^2}`{=tex}. \]

For (C(4,8)) and (A(-1,7)): \[ d(C,A) =
`\sqrt{(4-(-1))^2+(8-7)^2}`{=tex} \] \[ =`\sqrt{25+1}`{=tex}
=`\sqrt{26}`{=tex}. \]

For (C(4,8)) and (B(5,3)): \[ d(C,B) = `\sqrt{(4-5)^2+(8-3)^2}`{=tex} \]
\[ =`\sqrt{1+25}`{=tex} =`\sqrt{26}`{=tex}. \]

Therefore \[ d(C,A)=d(C,B). \]

So \[
`\boxed{\text{Yes, }C\text{ lies on the perpendicular bisector of }\overline{AB}.}`{=tex}
