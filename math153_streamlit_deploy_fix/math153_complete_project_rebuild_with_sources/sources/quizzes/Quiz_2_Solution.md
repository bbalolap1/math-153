## Q.1

\[ `\frac{\sqrt{a^4b^6}}`{=tex} {`\sqrt{a^{12}b^8}`{=tex}} \]

Combine the radicals:

\[ `\sqrt{a^{4-12}b^{6-8}}`{=tex} = `\sqrt{a^{-8}b^{-2}}`{=tex}. \]

Because this is an even root, absolute value is required:

\[ `\boxed{|a^{-4}b^{-1}|}`{=tex}. \]

Equivalent forms given in the source include

\[ `\boxed{a^{-4}|b^{-1}|}`{=tex} \]

and

\[ `\boxed{\frac1{a^4|b|}}`{=tex}. \]

## Q.2

The printed question

\[ x^2-y^2-6x-9 \]

contains a typo and was cancelled.

The intended question was

\[ x^2-y^2-6y-9. \]

Complete the square in (y):

\[ x^2-(y^2+6y+9) \]

\[ =x^2-(y+3)^2. \]

Difference of squares:

\[ `\boxed{(x-y-3)(x+y+3)}`{=tex}. \]

## Q.3

\[ `\frac{x^3-2x^2}`{=tex} {x\^2-3x-10}. \]

Factor the numerator:

\[ x^3-2x^2=x\^2(x-2). \]

Factor the denominator:

\[ x\^2-3x-10 = x\^2-5x+2x-10 \]

\[ =x(x-5)+2(x-5) \]

\[ =(x+2)(x-5). \]

Thus

\[ `\boxed{
\frac{x^2(x-2)}
{(x+2)(x-5)}
}`{=tex} \]

with no common factor to cancel.

## Q.4

\[ `\frac1{x+1}`{=tex} - `\frac1{x+2}`{=tex} +
`\frac3{(x+1)(x+2)}`{=tex}. \]

LCD:

\[ (x+1)(x+2). \]

Rewrite:

\[ `\frac{x+2}{(x+1)(x+2)}`{=tex} - `\frac{x+1}{(x+1)(x+2)}`{=tex} +
`\frac3{(x+1)(x+2)}`{=tex}. \]

Combine:

\[ `\frac{x+2-(x+1)+3}`{=tex} {(x+1)(x+2)} \]

# \[

`\boxed{
\frac4{(x+1)(x+2)}
}`{=tex}. \]

## Bonus

\[ `\frac1{\sqrt[3]{a}-\sqrt[3]{b}}`{=tex}. \]

Use the difference-of-cubes identity:

\[ u^3-v^3=(u-v)(u^2+uv+v^2). \]

Here

\[ u=`\sqrt[3]{a}`{=tex}, `\qquad`{=tex} v=`\sqrt[3]{b}`{=tex}. \]

Multiply numerator and denominator by

\[ `\sqrt[3]{a^2}`{=tex} + `\sqrt[3]{ab}`{=tex} + `\sqrt[3]{b^2}`{=tex}.
\]

Then the denominator becomes

\[ a-b. \]

Therefore

\[ `\boxed{
\frac{
\sqrt[3]{a^2}+\sqrt[3]{ab}+\sqrt[3]{b^2}
}{a-b}
}`{=tex}. \]
