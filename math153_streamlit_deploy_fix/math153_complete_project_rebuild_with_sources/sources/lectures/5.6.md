# 5.6 --- Exponential and Logarithmic Equations

## Change-of-Base Formula

The notes give

\[ `\boxed{
\log_b(a)=\frac{\log_c(a)}{\log_c(b)}
}`{=tex} \]

for positive (a,b,c), with (b,c`\ne1`{=tex}).

Two particularly useful versions are

\[ `\boxed{
\log_b(a)=\frac{\ln(a)}{\ln(b)}
}`{=tex} \]

and

\[ `\boxed{
\log_b(a)=\frac{\log(a)}{\log(b)}
}`{=tex}. \]

## Example --- Approximate (`\log`{=tex}\_2(5))

Using common logarithms:

\[ `\log`{=tex}\_2(5)=`\frac{\log(5)}{\log(2)}`{=tex} \]

and the notes obtain

\[ `\boxed{\log_2(5)\approx2.32}`{=tex}. \]

Using natural logarithms:

\[ `\log`{=tex}\_2(5)=`\frac{\ln(5)}{\ln(2)}`{=tex} \]

gives the same approximation:

\[ `\boxed{\log_2(5)\approx2.32}`{=tex}. \]

# Solving Exponential Equations

## Example 1

Solve

\[ 3\^x=21. \]

Take logarithm base (3):

\[ `\log`{=tex}\_3(3\^x)=`\log`{=tex}\_3(21). \]

Therefore

\[ x=`\log`{=tex}\_3(21). \]

Using change of base,

\[ x=`\frac{\ln(21)}{\ln(3)}`{=tex}. \]

The notes approximate:

\[ `\boxed{x\approx2.77}`{=tex}. \]

## Example 2

Solve

\[ 5^{2x+1}=6^{x-2}. \]

Take natural logarithms:

\[ `\ln`{=tex}(5\^{2x+1}) = `\ln`{=tex}(6\^{x-2}). \]

Use the power rule:

\[ (2x+1)`\ln`{=tex}(5) = (x-2)`\ln`{=tex}(6). \]

Expand:

\[ 2`\ln`{=tex}(5)x+`\ln`{=tex}(5) = `\ln`{=tex}(6)x-2`\ln`{=tex}(6). \]

Collect (x)-terms:

\[ 2`\ln`{=tex}(5)x-`\ln`{=tex}(6)x = -2`\ln`{=tex}(6)-`\ln`{=tex}(5).
\]

Factor:

\[ x(2`\ln`{=tex}(5)-`\ln`{=tex}(6)) = -2`\ln`{=tex}(6)-`\ln`{=tex}(5).
\]

Therefore

\[ `\boxed{
x=
\frac{-2\ln(6)-\ln(5)}
{2\ln(5)-\ln(6)}
}`{=tex}. \]

The notes approximate

\[ `\boxed{x\approx-3.64}`{=tex}. \]
