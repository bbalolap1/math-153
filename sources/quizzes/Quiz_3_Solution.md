## Q.1 --- Completing the Square

Express

\[ 4x\^2+8x+5 \]

in completed-square form.

Factor (4) from the (x)-terms:

\[ 4x\^2+8x+5 = 4(x\^2+2x)+5. \]

Since

\[ x^2+2x+1=(x+1)^2, \]

add and subtract (1):

\[ 4(x\^2+2x+1-1)+5. \]

Thus

\[ 4((x+1)\^2-1)+5 \]

\[ =4(x+1)\^2-4+5 \]

\[ `\boxed{4(x+1)^2+1}`{=tex}. \]

## Q.2 --- Two-Way Radios

Two children have radios with a maximum range of

\[ 2`\text{ miles}`{=tex}. \]

One leaves at 1:00 P.M., walking north at

\[ 4`\text{ mi/hr}`{=tex}. \]

The other leaves the same point at 1:15 P.M., traveling south at

\[ 6`\text{ mi/hr}`{=tex}. \]

The first child has a 15-minute head start:

\[ 15`\text{ min}`{=tex}=0.25`\text{ hr}`{=tex}. \]

Distance covered during the head start:

\[ (4)(0.25)=1`\text{ mile}`{=tex}. \]

Let (t) be the number of hours after 1:15 P.M.

Their separation is

\[ (4t+1)+6t=10t+1. \]

Set the separation equal to the radio range:

\[ 10t+1=2. \]

Then

\[ 10t=1 \]

\[ t=0.1`\text{ hr}`{=tex}. \]

Convert to minutes:

\[ 0.1(60)=6`\text{ minutes}`{=tex}. \]

Therefore they reach maximum range at

\[ `\boxed{1:21\text{ P.M.}}`{=tex} \]

The diagram on page 1 shows the first child north of the starting point
with the 1-mile head start, moving north at (4) mi/hr, while the second
child moves south at (6) mi/hr.

## Q.4 --- Absolute Value Equation

\[ 2\|5x+2\|-1=5. \]

\[ 2\|5x+2\|=6. \]

Divide by (2):

\[ \|5x+2\|=3. \]

\[ 5x+2=3 \]

or

\[ 5x+2=-3. \]
