## Q.1 --- Circle

General equation: \[ `\boxed{(x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=r^2}`{=tex}. \]

Given: \[ x^2-4x+y^2=5. \]

Complete the square: \[ (x^2-4x)+y^2=5 \] \[ (x-2)^2-4+y^2=5 \] \[
(x-2)^2+y^2=9 \] \[ `\boxed{(x-2)^2+(y-0)^2=3^2}`{=tex}. \]

Center: \[ `\boxed{(2,0)}`{=tex} \]

Radius: \[ `\boxed{3}`{=tex}. \]

## Q.2 --- Perpendicular Line

The given line \[ y=1-x \] has slope \[ -1. \]

A perpendicular line has slope \[ m=1. \]

Point-slope form through ((-1,4)): \[ y-4=1(x+1). \]

Solve for (y): \[ `\boxed{y=x+5}`{=tex}. \]

## Q.3 --- Domain

\[ f(x)=`\frac{\sqrt{x+1}}{2x}`{=tex}. \]

Square-root restriction: \[ x+1`\ge0`{=tex} \] so \[ x`\ge-1`{=tex}. \]

Denominator restriction: \[ 2x`\ne0`{=tex} \] so \[ x`\ne0`{=tex}. \]

Therefore \[ `\boxed{[-1,0)\cup(0,\infty)}`{=tex}. \]
