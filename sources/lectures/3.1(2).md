## Page 8 --- General Equation of the Perpendicular Bisector

The notes ask:

> Can we find a general equation in (x) and (y) for the points on the
> perpendicular bisector of (A(1,7)) and (B(-3,2))?

Let

\[ P(x,y) \]

be a point on the perpendicular bisector.

Then:

\[ d(A,P)=d(B,P). \]

So:

\[ `\sqrt{(x-1)^2+(y-7)^2}`{=tex} = `\sqrt{(x-(-3))^2+(y-2)^2}`{=tex}.
\]

Square both sides:

\[ (x-1)^2+(y-7)^2 = (x+3)^2+(y-2)^2. \]

Expand:

\[ x^2-2x+1+y^2-14y+49 = x^2+6x+9+y^2-4y+4. \]

Cancel (x\^2) and (y\^2), then collect terms:

\[ -8x-10y+37=0. \]

Standard form:

\[ `\boxed{-8x-10y=-37}`{=tex}. \]
