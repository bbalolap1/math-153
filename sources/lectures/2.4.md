## Page 1 --- The Complex Number System

The **complex number system** is an extension of the real number system
containing square roots of negative numbers.

Define

\[ `\boxed{i^2=-1}`{=tex}. \]

A complex number has the form

\[ `\boxed{a+bi}`{=tex}, \]

where (a) and (b) are real numbers.

-   \(a\) = real part
-   \(b\) = coefficient of the imaginary part

The page includes a complex-plane diagram:

-   horizontal axis = **Real**
-   vertical axis = **Imaginary**

Points such as

\[ 1+i,`\quad 2`{=tex}+i,`\quad -1`{=tex}+2i,`\quad 1`{=tex}+3i \]

are plotted according to their real and imaginary components.

### Powers of (i)

Powers of (i) follow a cyclic pattern:

\[ i\^1=i \]

\[ i\^2=-1 \]

\[ i\^3=-i \]

\[ i\^4=1 \]

and then the pattern repeats every four powers.

# Complex Conjugates and Rationalizations

If

\[ z=a+bi, \]

then its conjugate is

\[ `\boxed{\bar z=a-bi}`{=tex}. \]

Examples:

\[ 5+7i`\longleftrightarrow5`{=tex}-7i \]

\[ -1-2i`\longleftrightarrow-1`{=tex}+2i \]

\[ 3=3+0i`\longleftrightarrow3`{=tex}-0i=3 \]

\[ 3i=0+3i`\longleftrightarrow0`{=tex}-3i=-3i \]

The conjugate of the conjugate is the original number:

\[ `\boxed{\overline{\bar z}=z}`{=tex} \]

## Page 6 --- Special Property of Conjugates

\[ (a+bi)(a-bi)=a^2+b^2 \]

Thus

\[ z`\bar `{=tex}z \]

is a positive real number when (z`\ne0`{=tex}).

### Using conjugates to simplify a complex quotient

Simplify:

\[ `\frac{7-i}{3-5i}`{=tex} \]

The goal is to express the result as

\[ a+bi \]

with (a,b) real.

The conjugate of the denominator is

\[ 3+5i. \]

Multiply numerator and denominator by it:

\[ `\frac{7-i}{3-5i}`{=tex} `\cdot`{=tex} `\frac{3+5i}{3+5i}`{=tex} \]

Denominator:

\[ (3-5i)(3+5i)=3^2+5^2=34. \]
